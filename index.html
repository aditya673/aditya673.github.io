Aditya Dwivedi

I am a first‑year PhD student in Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay.

My research interests lie in commutative algebra (in particular \(F\)-singularities), homological algebra, category theory, K‑theory, and transcendental number theory.

I completed a BS‑MSc dual degree in Mathematics from IIT Bombay (CPI: 9.4/10, Mathematics: 9.8/10).
